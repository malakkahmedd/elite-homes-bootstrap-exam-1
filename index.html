Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to the most common questions about buying, selling, and
investing in real estate

You're ready to buy a home when you have stable income, a healthy credit score, saved enough for a down payment (typically 3-20%), and plan to stay in the area for at least 3-5 years.

Spring and early summer (April through June) are traditionally the best times to sell. Buyers are active, weather is good for viewings, and families want to move before the new school year.

Your offer should be based on recent comparable sales in the neighborhood, the property's condition, market competitiveness, and your agent's advice rather than just the asking price.

Closing costs are fees associated with processing and closing your mortgage (typically 2-5% of loan value). Both buyers and sellers have closing costs, though buyers usually cover lender-related fees.
